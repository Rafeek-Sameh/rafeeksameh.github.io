About Me

Hello, I'm Rafeek Sameh, a data analyst who helps business owners and companies turn scattered data into clear, number-backed decisions. I work across the entire analytics cycle, from start to finish:

  • Data extraction: writing SQL queries (filtering, Group By, Joins, CASE, Subqueries) and designing normalized relational databases.
  • Data cleaning: removing duplicates, handling missing values, standardizing dates/currencies, and splitting or merging columns using Power Query, Excel, and SQL.
  • Data modeling: building table relationships, date hierarchies, and calculated columns.
  • Analysis: DAX measures such as total sales, profit margin, YOY and MOM growth, running totals, and moving averages.
  • Presentation: interactive Power BI dashboards with slicers, drill-down detail pages, and a professional design.

Tell me about your data and what you want to learn from it, and I'll suggest the best solution.
